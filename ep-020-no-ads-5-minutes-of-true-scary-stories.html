Story 1 of 1 · 3 min readThe Third Cup▶ Hear this part

I moved out to that place near the county line because I wanted the silence. It was a small house with a narrow hallway that connected the bedroom to the kitchen. The floorboards were old pine, and they always felt a little cold under my feet in the morning.

I live alone, so I have a very set routine. I usually wake up at six, walk down that hall, and start the kettle before I even put my glasses on. But one Tuesday, I walked out and saw a cup sitting on the small side table halfway to the kitchen.

It was a ceramic mug I hadn't used in months, filled with hot herbal tea. I could see the steam rising in the dim light of the hall. I stood there for a long time just looking at it, trying to remember if I had made it during the night.

I poured it down the sink and told myself I was just tired or sleepwalking. The next morning, I woke up an hour later than usual, around seven. The cup was there again, in the exact same spot on the table, still steaming.

I started locking my bedroom door from the inside, which felt silly because I was the only one in the house. I even checked the windows every night before I went to bed. Everything was always latched tight, just the way I left it.

On Thursday, I decided to stay awake to see who was coming into the hall. I sat in a chair facing the bedroom door with the lights off. I didn't hear a single sound all night, not even a floorboard creaking in the rest of the house.

When the sun started to come up, I finally opened my door to go to the kitchen. The mug was sitting there on the little table, perfectly centered on the wood. It smelled like chamomile and something else I couldn't quite place, like dried leaves.

I know how this sounds, and I tried to find a logical reason for it. I thought maybe the previous owner had a key, but I had changed the locks the day I moved in. I even checked the dust on the floorboards, but there were no footprints besides my own.

One night I left a note on the table next to where the cup usually appeared. I just wrote 'Please stop' on a piece of yellow lined paper. I didn't sleep well that night, listening to the house settle and the wind in the chimney.

In the morning, the note was gone, but the tea was there as usual. The mug was so hot I could barely pick it up to move it. I looked in all the trash cans, but I never found that piece of yellow paper.

I started sleeping with the light on, but it didn't change anything at all. Every morning, regardless of when I opened my door, that third cup was waiting for me. It was always full, always hot, and always the same pale blue ceramic mug.

I stopped pouring the tea out after a while and just started leaving it there. By the time I get home from work in the afternoon, the cup is always gone. It's back in the cupboard, clean and dry, tucked behind the bowls where I don't usually look.

I’ve thought about moving, but I don't really have anywhere else to go right now. The house is quiet, and nothing else ever happens that feels out of the ordinary. It’s just that one thing in the hallway every morning.

I woke up a few minutes ago and I can hear the faint sound of a spoon clinking against ceramic. I'm just going to stay here in bed for a little while longer. The tea will be waiting for me when I finally decide to get up.

The end
