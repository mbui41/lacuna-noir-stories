Story 1 of 1The Blue Suitcase

I live in a house at the end of a cul-de-sac. It is a quiet place where the only sound is the refrigerator hum and the wind in the vents. I have lived there alone for three years now.

It started on a Tuesday in November. I came down the stairs around six in the morning to make coffee. There was a blue suitcase sitting right by the front door, standing perfectly upright.

I thought maybe I had left it there after my last trip, but that was months ago. I moved it back into the hall closet and shut the door. I didn't think much of it, honestly.

The next morning, it was back in the exact same spot. It was a hard-shell suitcase, a dusty shade of blue, with a small silver tag hanging from the handle. I checked the lock on the front door, and it was still bolted.

I know how this sounds, but I opened the suitcase that time. It was packed full of men’s clothes, all folded neatly. They weren't my clothes, though they looked like they would fit me.

There were wool sweaters and heavy trousers, all smelling faintly of cedar and cold air. I put the suitcase in the garage this time, behind a stack of old moving boxes. I made sure the garage door was locked tight.

I stayed up late that night watching a movie, just sitting on the sofa. I didn't hear a single sound, not even a floorboard creaking. When I got up to go to bed at midnight, the suitcase was back by the door.

I felt a bit cold then, even though the heat was on. I didn't move it that night; I just walked past it and went upstairs. I lay in bed listening to the house settle, but it was completely silent.

In the morning, the suitcase was gone. The space by the door was empty, and there wasn't even a scuff mark on the wood. I checked the garage, and the spot behind the boxes was empty too.

I went to work and tried to tell myself I was just tired or forgetting things. But when I came home, the house felt heavier, like someone had been standing in the rooms while I was away. The air smelled like that cedar again.

That night, I put a heavy chair in front of the door. I sat in the kitchen and drank a glass of water, just waiting. I must have drifted off at the table for a few minutes.

When I woke up, the chair had been moved back to the wall. The blue suitcase was there again, but this time it was open. A single coat was laid out across the back of the sofa, as if someone was coming back for it.

I don't bother moving it anymore. Every morning it is there by the door, and every evening it is gone. I just walk around it now when I leave for work.

I sometimes wonder if the clothes inside are getting thinner, or if more are being added. I haven't looked inside for a long time. I just leave the porch light on and go to sleep.

I think the smells are changing now. When I come home from my shift, the hallway doesn't smell like my coffee or my old boots anymore. It smells like a rainy wool blanket, something damp and heavy that's been kept in a dark closet for a long time.

Last Tuesday, I heard a sound from the living room while I was brushing my teeth. It was just a soft click, like a latch being pressed down very gently. I stayed in the bathroom for twenty minutes until the house went back to being completely silent.

I don't go into the living room after dark anymore. I just stay in my bedroom with the door shut tight. I can hear the floorboards in the hall settling under a weight that isn't mine. I usually just close my eyes and wait for morning.
