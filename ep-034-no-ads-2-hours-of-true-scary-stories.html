I’ve worked at the community pool for three years now. It’s a quiet job, mostly just checking chemical levels and making sure the kids don’t run on the wet tile, which is fine by me.

Closing time is my favorite part of the shift because the noise finally stops. The echoes of splashing and shouting die down, leaving just the low hum of the filtration system beneath the floorboards.

It was a Tuesday night, I think, right around 9:15 PM. I remember looking at my watch and thinking about the leftovers I had waiting in my fridge back at the apartment.

I started my final walkthrough, which is just a standard safety protocol we do every night. I have to check the locker rooms, the sauna, and then walk the entire perimeter of the pool deck one last time.
