Sometimes I wake up and my sheets feel heavy, like they're soaked through. But when I touch them, they are always crisp and cool. I haven't gone back to that job, but I still have the photo on my phone.

I don't look at it often because the time on the clock in the picture changes. Every time I open the gallery, it's a few minutes closer to now. I just leave the phone face down on the nightstand.
