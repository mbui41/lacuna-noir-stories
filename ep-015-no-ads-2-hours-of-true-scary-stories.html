I picked this spot because it was cheap and far away from anyone else. It was just a small clearing at the edge of an old pine forest, about three hours from the nearest city. I parked my truck, pitched my tent, and felt the silence sink in immediately.

I went to bed early that first night, probably around nine o'clock. I remember lying on my sleeping pad and listening for anything, maybe a coyote or a deer, but there was nothing. I fell into a very deep, dreamless sleep and didn't wake up until the sun hit the tent fabric.

I unfolded it and read the handwriting, which was very neat, like something an older person would write. It said, 'Please accept our deepest apologies for the noise from the gathering last night.' I stared at it for a minute, confused, because I hadn't heard a single thing.
